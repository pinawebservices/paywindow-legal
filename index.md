# Privacy Policy for PayWindow

**Last updated: November 2026**

This policy covers both the PayWindow iOS app and the PayWindow website (including its email newsletter signup). The two are handled very differently — read on for specifics.

## In the App

### The short version

PayWindow does not collect, transmit, store, or sell your financial data or personal details such as your name or email. Everything you enter into the app — income, expenses, savings goals, and paycheck schedules — stays on your device. We never ask for your bank login, and we never see what you enter.

Three things do leave your device, and none of them includes your financial data: (1) optional, anonymous feedback, if you answer a short survey while on a free trial (for example, why you chose to cancel, or how the trial is going) — see "Optional feedback surveys" below; (2) anonymous information about the setup screens you see when you first open the app, including your answers to its two multiple-choice questions — see "Setup screens and questions" below; and (3) the information needed to run your subscription, which also includes those two answers — see "Subscriptions and purchases" below.

### What data PayWindow stores

PayWindow stores the following information locally on your device only, using Apple's on-device SwiftData storage:

- Income sources and amounts you enter
- Expenses and bills you enter, including due dates and recurrence
- Savings goals and contribution settings you configure
- Paycheck schedule and window preferences

This data is never transmitted to us, to any third party, or to any server. PayWindow has no cloud sync and no user accounts — your data exists only on the device(s) where you've installed the app. (The only information the app sends is the optional survey feedback, the setup screen information and the subscription information described below.)

### What PayWindow does not do

- We do not connect to your bank. PayWindow never asks for, stores, or has access to any bank account credentials, card numbers, or financial account access of any kind.
- We do not run advertising or cross-app tracking. PayWindow has no ads, does not use your advertising identifier, and does not follow you across other apps and websites. Apart from which setup screens you see when you first open the app (see "Setup screens and questions" below), it does not record what you do inside the app. The only third-party service built into the app is RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions (see "Subscriptions and purchases" below).
- We do not sell your data. The only data we receive is the optional, anonymous survey feedback, the setup screen information and the subscription information described below, and we do not sell it or share it for advertising.
- We do not require an account or login to use the app. There is no in-app sign-up, no email collection within the app, and no user account system. (If you sign up for our email newsletter on our website, see "On This Website" below — that's a separate, optional thing, unrelated to the app itself.)

### Optional feedback surveys

If you start a free trial of PayWindow Premium, the app may ask you a short question: how the trial is going, or why you're canceling. Answering is entirely optional, and you can skip or dismiss any of them.

- **What's sent:** only your answer, any comment you choose to type, which survey it was (check-in or cancellation), how many days into the trial you were, which subscription plan the trial was for, and the app version.
- **What's not sent:** your name, email address, device identifier, Apple ID, or any income, expense, or savings information. Responses are anonymous — we can't tell which user wrote one.
- **Where it goes:** to a database we host with Supabase, a cloud database provider. Like any online service, it may process technical connection details (such as an IP address) to deliver the request; we don't use that to identify you.
- **How we use it:** only to understand how to improve PayWindow. It isn't sold, shared for advertising, or used to track you.
- **If you're offline:** a response may be saved briefly on your device and sent the next time the app can connect.

Because responses are anonymous, we can't look up or delete one on request. Please don't include personal information in comments.

### Setup screens and questions

When you first open PayWindow, a few setup screens walk you through adding your income and first expense, and ask two multiple-choice questions: how you plan your budget today, and what you find hardest about managing money between paydays. To learn where people get stuck and which kinds of users find PayWindow useful, the app sends:

- **To our Supabase database (anonymous):** which setup screens you viewed and finished, your answers to the two questions, whether setup ended with a purchase or free trial (or a restored purchase), and the app version. These events are grouped by a random code that the app keeps on your device until setup is finished, so that starting setup over counts as one attempt rather than two. The code isn't connected to your identity, your device's identifiers or your subscription, so we can't tell who sent them. Like any online service, the database may process technical connection details (such as an IP address) to deliver the request; we don't use that to identify you.
- **To RevenueCat (with your subscription record):** your answers to the two questions, so we can see whether people who answered differently go on to keep their subscription. See "Subscriptions and purchases" below.
- **Never sent:** the amounts, dates, names or any other information you type into the setup screens. Those stay on your device like everything else you enter.

None of this is used for advertising, sold, or used to track you across other apps.

### Subscriptions and purchases

PayWindow is a subscription app. Purchases, free trials and renewals are handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see or store your payment card or Apple ID details.

To confirm whether your subscription is active, and to let you restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives:

- an anonymous identifier it generates for the app installation (not your name, email or Apple ID),
- your purchase and subscription status (which plan, when it started, when it renews or ends, whether you are in a free trial),
- your answers to the two setup questions (how you plan your budget today, and what you find hardest about managing money between paydays),
- basic device and app information (such as the app version, device model, operating system version, and country), and
- your IP address, which is part of any internet request.

RevenueCat does not receive any of the income, expense, savings or schedule information you enter into PayWindow. We use this information only to give you access to what you purchased, to restore purchases, and to understand subscription performance in aggregate, including how it differs by setup answer. It is not used for advertising or sold. RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

We also keep a small flag on your device recording whether you were an early user of PayWindow, based on when the app was first installed, so that early users keep Premium without a subscription. That check is made on your device using information Apple provides to the app.

### Notifications

PayWindow uses Apple's local notification system (UNUserNotificationCenter) to remind you of upcoming bills, and, during a free trial, to optionally ask how it's going. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device and do not involve any external push notification service or server.

## On This Website

Our website (where you can learn about and download the app) offers an optional email newsletter so you can hear about new features and updates.

- If you choose to sign up, we collect only your email address, via Mailchimp, our email service provider.
- Your email is used solely to send you occasional product updates — never sold, shared, or used for anything else.
- You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.
- Signing up is entirely optional and has no effect on your ability to use the PayWindow app.

## Children's privacy

PayWindow is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children within the app (including survey feedback), and the website's newsletter signup is not directed at or intended for children.

## Changes to this policy

If PayWindow's data practices change in the future (for example, if optional iCloud sync is introduced), this policy will be updated accordingly, and any such feature will be clearly disclosed and will not be required to use the app's core functionality.
